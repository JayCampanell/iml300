Reflection

I had vibecoded before but that was for code that had a specific output that I knew, it was some factual statement not a design piece. Shifting my idea of what vibecoding could be used for showed me more than I could imagine, somehow without a clear output which could be checked against the truth, I had to be much more intentional with what I wanted. I had to specify every detail when prompting instead of giving a problem statement. I think when it comes to technology vibecoding is a good thing when it comes to using technology for design. It lowers the barrier to entry especially when making websites but that being said, it only knows what it is trained on and to create something truly great you need a human to have a vision because without a human then everyone website will be filled with gradients and italic text.
